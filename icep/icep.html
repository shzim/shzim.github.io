International Delegation

ICEP Maldives 2019

International Cadet Exchange Programme (Maldives NCC Camp - XXI)

Programme Overview

The International Cadet Exchange Programme (ICEP) facilitates youth delegation exchanges across nations to foster international cooperation, leadership, and cultural understanding. Representing Bangladesh as an official delegate from the Ministry of Defense, Shariar Hossain Zim participated in joint training exercises, cultural exchange programs, and official diplomatic engagements with officers of the Maldives National Cadet Corps (MNCC).

Highlights & Official Meetings

  • Official delegation photo session with Brig Gen Abdul Baten Khan (DG, BNCC).
  • Warm reception by Maldives National Cadet Corps officers at Velana International Airport.
  • Strategic schedule briefings & presentations at Iskandhar School, Malé, Maldives.
  • High-level meetings with DG, MNCC and Commanding Officer, MNCC.
